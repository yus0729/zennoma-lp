25min

Breathe, focus, and begin again.

  1. Breathe

    Breathe in slowly, then breathe out even more slowly. Repeat six times. A longer exhale helps your body settle. The circle is drawn in time with your breathing.

  2. Focus

    Give one chosen task a set amount of focused time. Instead of counting down, the timer counts up, reflecting the value of building your progress little by little.

  3. Repeat

    When you finish, take a pause, then begin again with breathing. The results of your focused effort quietly build on one another.

Plan your day,
then put it into practice.

Bring focus, meditation and other time blocks together in one schedule, so you can always see how you want to spend your day. ZENnoMA supports you in putting your ideal day into practice.

Save your regular schedules as templates. The app and web stay in sync, so you can see the same plan in both.

Make time
to sit quietly.

Sitting quietly helps bring your body, breath and mind into balance. Valuing the here and now through meditation can also support your ability to focus.

When you're distracted
or unsettled, breathe.

Too much time scrolling. Anger about to spill out. A sense of unease. It happens to everyone. Start breathing straight away from the app, a widget or your Lock Screen.

Gently close
what pulls you away.

On iPhone, you can keep selected apps closed during focus, meditation and scheduled time blocks. On the web, you can also block websites you specify. When you really need access on iPhone, take a breathing pause, then open the app for just five minutes.

Website blocking requires the Chrome or Edge extension. Five-minute temporary access after breathing is available on iPhone.
