Why ZENnoMA

When you can't focus,it isn't a lack of willpower.You just haven't settled yet.

Some apps turn focus into a game. Others lock your phone. ZENnoMA does something that comes before both. It settles you first, with ninety seconds of breathing, and then you begin.

25min

Breathe, focus, and begin again.

  1. Breathe

    Breathe in for five seconds and out for eight, six times. A longer exhale slows the body down. You don't need to count. The circle is drawn with each breath.

  2. Focus

    Twenty-five, forty-five or ninety minutes on the one thing you chose. There is no countdown, only a thin ring that slowly fills.

  3. Again

    When it ends, rest, then come back in through the breath. The time you focused adds up, just as it is.

Plan the day, then simply begin.

Put focus, meditation and the rest of your day on one schedule. When the time comes, “This hour” shows what's next, and one tap starts the breath.

Templates for your usual days. Calendar events beside your plan on iPhone, kept on your device. The same plan on iPhone and the web.

A quiet place to sit.

Meditation lives in the same app. Choose how long, choose a sound such as rain or a river, and sit with your natural breath.

When you drift, breathe.

Lost in your feed? It happens to everyone. Take ninety seconds from the app, a widget or your Lock Screen, then go back to what you planned.

Close the apps that pull you away.

On iPhone, the apps you choose stay closed while you focus or meditate, and during the times you set. If you really need one, breathe first, then open it.

What we leave out

There are no streaks, badges or rankings. There are no ads, and we don't analyze how you use the app. What you see is how long you focused today and this week. That's all.

Your calendar, Health data and the apps you choose to close stay on your device. Your plans and records sync only to your own account. Privacy Policy
