Focus begins
with a breath.

You can't focus not because your will is weak,
but because your state isn't settled yet.

ZENnoMA settles you with a 90-second breath, then begins your focus. Not a game, not a cage — a settled start.

Open the web app App Store — coming soon

How it works

  1. 01

    Breathe

    90 seconds — inhale for 5, exhale for 8, six times. Your state settles before you begin.

  2. 02

    Focus

    25, 45 or 90 minutes on the one thing you chose. Meditation sessions are here too.

  3. 03

    Repeat

    Pause, then begin again. The breath is always the way in.

What's inside

  • A breath when you drift

    Lost in social media? Take a 90-second breath from the app, the widget or your Lock Screen, then go back to your plan.

  • Plan your day

    Lay out focus, meditation and time blocks. Templates for your usual days, and your calendar events shown alongside (iPhone).

  • Close distracting apps

    On iPhone, the apps you choose close only while you focus or meditate, or during the time blocks you set — and open again when they end.

  • Honest numbers

    See today's and this week's focus time as it is. No streaks to protect.

What we don't do

  • No games

    No streaks, badges, XP or leaderboards. Focus isn't a contest.

  • No ads, no tracking

    No advertising or analytics SDKs. We never sell your data.

  • Stays on your device

    Your calendar, Health data and Screen Time choices never leave your device.

The basic loop — a breath, a 25-minute focus and a 10-minute meditation — is free.
